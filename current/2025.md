 The Chinese Scholarship Committee Records at Bryn Mawr house administrative, financial, and admissions materials related to the college's Chinese scholarship, which operated throughout the 20th century. It also contains a strong collection of correspondences from Chinese scholars. Yet despite its significance in documenting Asian history at Bryn Mawr, the collection has been historically overlooked and minimally processed. 

As part of the 2025 “Who Built Bryn Mawr?” project, Chloe Sun ‘28 and Paulina Phan ‘28, advised by Allison Mills and Bethany Greenho, reprocessed these records. The process involved extensive historical research, physical reorganization, and other archival procedures. Their goal was to improve the collection's accessibility and increase scholar visibility, making it a better resource for students, researchers, and stakeholders. This blog documents and reflects upon their processing journey, from preliminary research to finding aid publication.

By resurfacing and reorganizing the Chinese Scholarship Committee Records, the Student Archivists hope to highlight the voices of individual scholars and recontextualize pre-existing organizational narratives. They also hope to compel a rethinking of who has been allowed to belong at Bryn Mawr and why, reviving conversations surrounding race, gender, and education at Bryn Mawr.
